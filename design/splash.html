JoME · motion design · for review

JoME wakes up and waters its garden.

This plays once on each cold start, right after the native launch screen, and hands over to the app in about 1.6 seconds. It uses the real logo SVG. Only existing parts are grouped so they can move; no shape is changed. The ripple uses water blue because water is flowing.

  1. 0.00 sHandoff. Identical to the native launch screen: mascot centred on the ground colour, so there's no jump.
  2. 0.08 sEyes blink awake.
  3. 0.22 sA water drop falls onto the leaf.
  4. 0.56 sIt lands. The sprout bounces and a ripple spreads out, twice.
  5. 0.62 sThe wordmark draws itself, letter by letter.
  6. 1.30 sZoom through and fade into the app.
  7. 1.65 sApp ready. The app keeps loading underneath the whole time, so the splash never adds waiting.

With reduced motion on, nothing moves: the logo holds for 0.4 s and fades out.
